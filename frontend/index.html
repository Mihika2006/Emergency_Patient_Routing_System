Emergency Patient Routing System

Three linked portals for a single patient transport session — pick one to open.

01 · VEHICLE

Driver Console

Live turn-by-turn navigation, route simulation, and 1-tap reroute confirmation when a patient's condition breaches a safety threshold.
02 · PATIENT

Paramedic Console

Stream heart rate, SpO2, and blood pressure to the destination ER, with automatic Red/Yellow/Green triage flagging.
03 · RECEIVING ER

Hospital ER Command Center

Track incoming ambulances, watch live vitals stream in, and manage bed & trauma bay readiness across the region.
